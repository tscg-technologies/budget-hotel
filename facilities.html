Our Maintenance Protocol

Cleanliness & Maintenance Practices

We believe a budget hotel must never compromise on tidiness. Our maintenance team follows a systematic multi-step protocol before any room is assigned to an arriving guest:

• Fresh Linen Exchange

Pillow covers, bedsheets, and towels are laundered and inspected between each guest stay.

• Bathroom Disinfection

Washroom fixtures, tiles, faucets, and floors are thoroughly scrubbed and disinfected daily.

• High-Touch Surface Care

Door handles, television remotes, electric switches, and desk surfaces are wiped down meticulously.

• Functional Checks

Electrical points, water taps, air conditioning, and lights are checked prior to check-in.

* Note: Information describes standard operational practices and does not imply third-party medical or star-rating hygiene certification.
